## Validated calibration statement

Fourteen historically conditioned DAO twins were trained on 2023-2024
profiles and evaluated on a separately hashed 2025 temporal holdout. Across
30 episodes of 1,440 steps per DAO, mean bounded composite similarity was
0.473. Calibrated simulations outperformed historical persistence for 5 of
14 DAOs and an uncalibrated simulator for 8 of 14. The result supports
DAO-dependent generative fidelity, not universal forecasting accuracy.
